## Direction contract

THESIS: The tool is the experiment screen. A calibrated mean-grey field where the white stimulus is the only bright object; refuses the dark IDE panel tile of every image-analysis tool.
OWN-WORLD: Mean-luminance grey #808080 field, darker grey bars #5C5C5C, white stimulus windows with no shadow, one red fixation-cross accent; all other chroma lives only inside stimuli and legends as data. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for UI, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for figures and R.
STORY: The researcher sees the drawing first, hovers a contour and reads its numbers at the cursor, switches stain or layer, and leaves with R code.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin top bar (name, drawing, layer switch); stimulus window ~80% of the field; floating readout beside the fixation cross; filmstrip library and histogram along the bottom.
FORM: Calibrated Grey Field, my list position 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed 0133a13f.
