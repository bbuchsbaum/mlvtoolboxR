# Selecting complete contours from a photograph

The two sources are the frozen Toronto FDoG and M-LSD vector drawings. Select whole paths and retain their existing segment coordinates and grouping. The cost is the number of distinct ink pixels added to the drawing, measured by the package rasterizer. Each photograph gets its own FDoG ink count as its maximum budget.

Use the existing within-category split ranks. Development fold is one plus (rank minus one) modulo five. Each fold holds out two photographs per category, twelve total, and all paths and artist labels from each held-out image are excluded from its fit. Fix the prospective reserve test to ranks 26 through 45 within each category. Open its 120 photos for prediction and scoring only if all development criteria pass.

Fit a single histogram gradient boosting regressor design, with 80 iterations, 12 leaves, minimum 50 training examples per leaf, learning rate 0.065 and L2 regularization 2. Inputs describe local contrast, shape, support from the other extractor, and contacts with neighboring paths. Recompute remaining ink cost, available area within two pixels, endpoint contact, and progress toward source T/X junctions as paths enter the drawing. The model sees no artist geometry, image identifier or category at inference.

At training time, measure newly covered artist ink per candidate within two pixels. A same-type source junction within four pixels of an artist junction supplies an additional eight pixels of training credit for retaining its member paths. Split half of this credit equally among its members and award the other half on completion. These labels guide selection only; final evaluation uses one-to-one matches on the actual R drawing. Predict marginal utility divided by the larger of newly covered neighborhood area and incremental ink. At selection time multiply this estimate by that same area and divide by incremental ink, then take the highest ranked feasible complete path. Break equal-score ties by larger incremental gain, then source order. Label training states at roughly 0%, 20%, 40%, 60% and 80% of the budget along two deterministic trajectories, a shuffled path order and a greedy choice using the training annotations. Weight photos equally, with square-root incremental cost weights within each photo. Do not sweep models or change the rule in response to held-out scores.

Pool the out-of-fold drawings from all 60 development photographs. Advance only when artist ink coverage improves by at least 0.02 absolute versus FDoG, contour F1 is at least FDoG's, both T and X matches are at least the original vector combination's, and every drawing is within budget. Report actual incident-contour agreement, detection counts, category results and paired photo uncertainty. On success refit on all 60, freeze the fit, and evaluate once on the designated reserve subset against contemporaneous controls. On failure retain the reserve for a different experiment.
